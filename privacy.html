Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Swipause: Five-Second Awareness (the "App") handles information.

Swipause is designed with privacy in mind. The App does not require user accounts, and we do not operate our own server to receive the practice-related information you create in the App. The information you enter and the settings you configure are designed to stay on your device.

However, the App uses certain device permissions and may interact with Apple App Store or Google Play services when you purchase or manage a subscription. Your device operating system and app store may independently process information under their own privacy policies.

This Policy explains what information is stored on your device, what information may be processed by third parties, and what choices you have.

By using Swipause, you acknowledge that you have been provided with this Privacy Policy. Where applicable law requires consent for a particular type of information processing, we will request that consent separately. This Policy does not replace any separate permission or consent required by law.

If you do not agree with the practices described in this Policy, please do not use the App.

2. What Is Stored on Your Device

So the App can work, it stores certain information locally on your device. This may include:

Your guarded-app list — The apps you choose to pause before opening. This information allows Swipause to identify which apps you have selected for the five-second practice.

Practice progress — Information about which day of the Five-Second Awareness seven-day practice you are on and which practices you have completed. This allows the App to maintain your progress between sessions.

Your loop names — The personal names you give your behavior loops through the "Name it" feature, along with information about how often each loop has been named. This information is used to display your chosen names and maintain relevant practice information.

Emergency-exit usage — Information about how many times you have used the emergency exit during the current day, so the App can apply the daily limit.

App settings — Your preferences and configuration, such as whether app guarding is switched on or off.

These categories of information are stored locally for the purpose of operating the App and providing its features.

We do not operate a server that receives your guarded-app list, loop names, or practice progress. We do not have remote access to this locally stored information.

Some of this information may relate to your personal habits or behavior. You should therefore treat it as private information, even though it is stored only on your device.

Device backups and system storage

Your device operating system may include application data in device backups or other system-managed storage, depending on your settings and the App's storage configuration.

3. Permissions the App Uses

To pause apps you have chosen to guard, Swipause uses device permissions and operating system capabilities that you authorize.

The specific permissions depend on your device and operating system.

On Android

Depending on the Android version and the App's implementation, Swipause may request permissions or special access such as:

  • Usage Access, to determine whether an app you selected is being opened.
  • Permission to display over other apps, where required to show the five-second practice interface.
  • Notification permission, if notifications are used by the version of the App you install.

These permissions are used to provide the app-guarding and practice features you configure.

On iOS

Depending on the App's implementation and the permissions approved by Apple, Swipause may use Apple's Screen Time technologies, including Family Controls and related system APIs.

These technologies allow the App to implement app-selection and app-restriction functionality within the capabilities and restrictions established by Apple.

Use of these technologies may require specific user authorization and the appropriate Apple developer entitlements.

How permissions are used

The App uses these permissions for the purpose of providing the app-guarding and five-second practice features you choose to enable.

The permissions are processed through the applicable operating system APIs. The App does not use them to build advertising profiles or to sell information about your app usage.

We do not operate our own server to receive the guarded-app information or practice information accessed through these permissions.

Your control over permissions

You can revoke permissions through your device settings at any time.

If you revoke a permission that Swipause needs, some or all app-guarding features may stop working.

The exact effect depends on your device, operating system, and the permission you revoke.

For more information about how your operating system handles these permissions, please consult Apple's or Google's applicable privacy and permission documentation.
