2. Description of Service

Swipause is a self-awareness and habit-practice tool.

When you try to open an app that you have chosen to guard, Swipause first presents a short practice — a five-second guided breathing exercise and a self-answer prompt ("Name it") drawn from the Five-Second Awareness seven-day practice — before allowing you to continue to the guarded app, subject to the capabilities and limitations of your device and operating system.

The Service includes:

  • App guarding: You select which installed apps are paused before they open, subject to the permissions and technical capabilities available on your device.
  • The five-second practice: A breathing timer and a self-answer prompt shown before a guarded app opens.
  • Seven days of practice content: The Five-Second Awareness guide, presented as daily cards.
  • Loop naming: You may give your own behavior loops personal names, which are shown back to you during the practice. Loop names are stored locally on your device.
  • Emergency exit: A feature that allows you to bypass the practice a limited number of times per day, subject to the App's settings and technical capabilities.
  • Guard controls: An option to turn off app guarding through the App's settings, subject to the proper functioning of the App and your device.

Swipause does not require you to create a user account. We do not operate a server that receives the User Content you create within the App. The App is designed to store its practice-related user data locally on your device.

However, your device's operating system, app store, backup services, or other third-party services may independently process information in accordance with their own terms and privacy policies. Please see our Privacy Policy for further details.

App guarding depends on device permissions, operating system features, and technical conditions. We do not guarantee that every selected app will be paused in every circumstance.

Swipause is not an emergency-access system, security system, or substitute for your device's built-in emergency functions. You should not rely on Swipause to control access to emergency services or other time-critical functions.
