Swipause: Five-Second Awareness

Open apps on purpose.

Swipause puts a five-second awareness practice in front of the apps that pull you in. Pick the apps that eat your day, and before they open, Swipause asks you to do one small thing first: breathe for five seconds, and name what is actually going on. Finish the practice, and your app opens.

No account. No sign-up. No server. Your guarded-app list, practice progress, and loop names live only on your device.
